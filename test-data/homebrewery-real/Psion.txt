## Psion
A large orc is flung across the room, smashing through tables and chairs before hitting the wall with
a thunderous crash, collapsing dazed. A human, her eyes still glowing with unearthly power, tosses a few extra coins on the bar. "Sorry for the mess. If he wakes up, tell him to try picking on someone his own size next time".

The prisoner's expression turns from defiant to
puzzled as the interrogator asks no questions, merely
stares at them silently. His puzzled expression turns
worried as he finds himself, unbidden, recalling where
he stashed the loot. "Under the stables behind the
Rusty Hook Inn" the interrogator finally speaks, as the
thief's expression becomes terrified.

"Ain't you... cold or somethin'?" the dwarf asks the
elf as they trudge through the snow, eying her simple
robe and bare feet. "I just think 'warm'" the elf replies
with a distracted air. The dwarf snorts, a puff of chilled
air. Typical elf nonsense. Except... they haven't frozen
to death yet. "Say... could you think some 'warm' this
way?" the dwarf asks hopefully. To the dwarf’s
surprise, the chill of the snow fades away completely.
"That's downright creepy... hey don't stop now! Was
jus' sayin' it was creepy."

Psions are those who have tapped into a special
otherworldly force, with the ability to actualize the
power of their mind to accomplish impossible feats.
Reading minds, lifting vast weights, and transcending
physical limitations, they tend to inspire awe and
terror in equal measure.

The exact nature of what psionic power is might be
a question answered in your setting, or it might not be.
Most view psionic power as coming from within,
though other answers exist - anything from the leaking
power of the Far Realm, to an alternate way to express
magic, to an actualization of an individual's will upon
the world.

### Powerful Minds
Since a psion's weapon is their mind, they are always a
careless thought away from harming those around them. For a
psion, the line between thinking and doing can be very thin,
forcing them to keep their thoughts and emotions in check,
lest their powers run amok. This leads to many of them
developing odd behaviors or mannerisms to help them control
their state of mind, frequently seeming quite eccentric to an
outside observer.

Consider how your psion keeps their powers in check, and
how much their power bleeds into their everyday life. How
reading minds may affect their body language or habits. Do
they add wood to the campfire from the comfort of their
bedroll, or do they restrain themselves from using their power
for trivial matters lest they slip up?

\column

### Misunderstood Power
In a world of widespread magic, many will assume a psion
to be a type of Sorcerer or Wizard, mistaking their powers for
the common applications of magic. The common villager
keeps only one mental category for the supernatural and
creepy people that bend reality to their will. But even among
magic users, a psion's powers will frequently be found
unnerving.

It is often those who do understand magic that find a psion’s
abilities the most aberrant. A force that intersects the magical
weave rather than obeys it, the nature of the power that the
psion wields one can only contemplate with unease.

### Creating a Psion
When creating a psion, consider your character's
background. How did you become a psion? Were you born
with latent powers? Or did something happen to you granting
your powers? Or did you intentionally train yourself through
rigorous mental exercise to leverage them? Consider how
developing a rare and mysterious power would have affected
your interactions with people.

Consider what your purpose is with your newfound powers,
or how you plan to use your powers. Do you intend to be taken
for a Wizard casting magic, or are you freely open about being
of a... different nature?

#### Quick Build
You can make a psion quickly by following these
suggestions. First, make Intelligence your highest ability
score, followed by Dexterity or Constitution. Second, choose
the acolyte, far traveler or hermit background.



{{note
##### Psionics & Magic
The mechanical interactions between magic and psionics are covered in the psionics feature, but may vary depending on your GM and setting. Psionics are still an expression of the supernatural, and may still be affected by effects that suppress magical effects, but are often mysterious and baffling to those familiar with magic, being a more raw interaction of the mind and weave.
}}

{{note
##### Psionics do not need to be psionic.

If the traditional idea of psychic powers does not fit your character or your setting, you are encouraged to reinterpret the Psion’s abilities however you wish.

Your Psi Points might represent an innate reservoir of magical power. Your Disciplines could instead be arcane techniques, supernatural gifts, divine manifestations, mutations, elemental powers, or some other extraordinary source.

A Psion can remain mechanically identical while its powers are described and understood completely differently within the story.

Work with your GM to determine what your powers are and how they fit into the setting.
}}

## Class Features

As a psion, you gain the following class features

#### Hit Points
**Hit Dice:**                    :: 1d6 per Psion level
**Hit Points at 1st Level:**     :: 6 + your Constitution modifier
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** :: 1d6 (or 4) + your Constitution modifier per Psion level after 1st

#### Proficiencies
**Armor:**   :: Light armor
**Weapons:** :: Simple weapons
**Tools:**   :: None

**Saving Throws:** :: Wisdom, Intelligence
**Skills:** :: Choose two from Arcana, Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, or Religion

#### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
- *(a)* a quarterstaff or *(b)* a dagger
- *(a)* leather armor
- *(a)* a scholar's pack or *(b)* an explorer's pack

### Psionic Archetype
At 1st level, you pick the archetype of psion you embody, choosing your archetype, each of which are detailed at the end of the class description. 

Your choice grants you features at 1st level, and again at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

### Psi points
Starting at 1st level, you gain access to psi points used to fuel
psionic discipline powers and effects. You have a number of
psi points equal to your psion level, and you regain all spent
points when you finish a short or long rest. You can spend a
number of psi points equal to your Proficiency Bonus at a time. 

### Psionic Discipline

At 2nd level, your Psionic Archetype grants you access to its associated Psionic Discipline. You choose this discipline before selecting the Psionic Talent you gain at this level.

A Psionic Discipline represents a broad field of psionic practice. Each discipline grants a passive feature, a signature psionic power, and access to Psionic Talents associated with that discipline.

A discipline's signature power can be modified and empowered by spending psi points, as described in that power. You cannot spend more psi points on a single use of a psionic power than your proficiency bonus, unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

#### Psionic Ability
Psionic powers and Psionic Talents gained through this class use your psionic ability.

**Psionic ability save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier::

**Psionic ability attack modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier::

#### Psionic Spellcasting

When a Psionic Discipline or Psionic Talent allows you to cast a spell, Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for that spell. 

You must maintain concentration on the spell as normal. 

\column

### Psionic Talents

Also at 2nd level, you learn one Psionic Talent of your choice. 

You learn one additional Psionic Talent when you reach 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th, 11th, 13th, 15th, 17th, and 19th level in this class, as shown in the Talents Known column of the Psion table.

You must meet all prerequisites for a talent when you select it. If a Psionic Talent has a level prerequisite, the prerequisite refers to your Psion level. If a talent requires a particular Psionic Discipline, you must know that discipline before you can select the talent.

Unless a talent states otherwise, you can select each Psionic Talent only once.

Whenever you gain a level in this class, you can replace one Psionic Talent you know with another Psionic Talent for which you meet all prerequisites. You cannot replace a talent that serves as a prerequisite for another talent you currently know unless you also replace or otherwise lose every talent that depends on it.




### Ability Score Improvement
When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1.

As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using
this feature.


### Psionic Mastery

Starting at 5th level, you gain 1 psi point at the start of each of your turns.

Any unspent mastery points are lost at the end of your turn. At 11th level, you gain 2 psi points at the start of each of your turns, and at 17th level, you gain 3.


### Second Discipline

At 9th level, your understanding of psionics expands beyond the practices of your original archetype. Choose a second Psionic Discipline from the list of Psionic Disciplines.

You cannot choose a discipline you already know. You gain the passive feature, signature power, and access to the Psionic Talents associated with the chosen discipline.
{{pageNumber,auto}}
\page

### Mystic Body

At 11th level, your psionic power permanently alters your body and mind. You no longer age, you are immune to disease, and you gain resistance to psychic damage.

In addition, you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.



### Third Discipline

At 18th level, your mastery of psionics allows you to develop expertise in another field of mental power. Choose a third Psionic Discipline from the list of Psionic Disciplines.

You cannot choose a discipline you already know. You gain the passive feature, signature power, and access to the Psionic Talents associated with the chosen discipline.

### Ascension

At 20th level, when you would die, you can instead transcend your physical form. You return to consciousness with 1 hit point, regain 10 expended psi points, and become incorporeal for 1 minute.

While incorporeal, you have a flying speed of 40 feet and can hover. You can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. If you end your turn inside an object, you take 1d10 force damage and are moved to the nearest unoccupied space. You have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage, and you can't be grappled, knocked prone, or restrained.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

##  Psionic Archetypes
### Awakened Mind
An Awakened Mind is a psion who had their psionic power
awakened within them by an encounter, event, or
circumstance. Perhaps their powers were latent or perhaps
their destiny had been that of a normal creature until powers
were suddenly thrust on them. 

Perhaps they brushed the Far
Realm in a vivid dream, perhaps they wandered the Feywild
and came back warped, perhaps they merely glimpsed the
eyes of a creature that did not belong walking down the street.

An awakening is often somewhat traumatic. Psions are not well understood and the first power they manifest - telepathy - is one of the most feared and shunned abilities.

An Awakened can be either empathetic or cruel, good or evil. Knowing what is on the minds of others affects people in different ways, driving some away from society as they see its true face, while others feel drawn to help the problems only they can see.


#### Mental Awareness
starting at 1st level, you can use Intelligence instead of Wisdom when making an Insight check against a creature with an Intelligence ability score of 6 or higher. 

Further, if you have telepathically communicated with a willing creature, you know their general location (direction and rough estimate of distance) for the next hour as long as you are on the same plane as them.

#### Opened Mind
At 2nd level when you select this archetype, your mind awakens the ability to directly connect to the minds of other creatures, granting the psionic discipline of Telepathy.

#### Mind Reader
Starting at 3rd level, when you use *Telepathic intrusion*, you can force the target to make an Intelligence saving throw instead of a Wisdom saving throw against the power (deciding when you use the ability).

When a creature fails a saving throw against your *Telepathic Intrusion*, you gain a d4. 

Until the end of your next turn, if you make an attack roll against the creature or the creature makes a saving throw against one of your psionic powers other than Telepathic Intrusion, you can add or subtract the d4 from the roll (deciding to roll before you roll the attack or use the power).

#### Empowered Psionics
Starting at 6th level, when you deal damage with a Psionic Discipline power you can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt.

\column

#### All Seeing Eye
Starting at 10th level, you can see a creature by its mind. You gain a mindsight of 60 feet, allowing you to see creatures with an intelligence of 6 or higher within range as if by blindsight.

A creature you are unaware of can still be hidden from you, but you can use your Intelligence modifier instead of your Wisdom modifier when making Perception checks to detect creatures.

#### Full Awakening
Starting at 14th level, you can briefly fully awaken your expanded mind to true comprehension. At the start of your turn, you can expend 2 psi points to gain advantage on all saving throws and attack rolls until the start of your next turn

### Consuming Mind
A dangerous branch of psionics that are born from the ability
to sap energy from other creatures, tearing from them their
thoughts, their mind, and ultimately their vitality for your own
consumption. A feared branch of power, it is often believed to
be one of the ancient primeval roots of psionic power as it is a
branch of power found most often the most ancient and
terrible of the practitioners of psionics, such as brain eating
squid faced monsters and ancient psionic space whales, which
might have other names in various settings.
It has been refined by those that seek greater power either
from ambition or desperation, and unlocks a terrible but
effective path. One example of mortal creatures walking this
path is the houses of dark elves, who developed their dark
powers from their study of feral vampiric brain eating
monsters.

#### Dark Lurker
At 1st level, your powers grant you intuition that
allows you to better adapt and survive as a mind hunter. You
gain proficiency in Stealth and Deception.

#### Psionic Predator
At 2nd level when you select this archetype, you gain the ability
to consume the psionic power of others. You gain the psionic
discipline of Consumption.

#### Ravenous Powers
Starting at 3rd level, you gain the Psionic Talent *Consumed Strength*, does not count against your Psionic Talents known, but cannot be switched out on leveling up.

Additionally, you gain proficiency in Athletics.

#### Empowered Psionics
Starting at 6th level, when you deal damage with a Psionic Discipline power you can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt.

\column

#### Mind Vampire
Starting at 10th level, as an action, you draw on the psychic energy of another creature you can see within 30 feet to sustain yourself.

The target must make a Charisma saving throw. On failure it takes 3d8 psychic damage and you
gain temporary hit points equal to half the amount of psychic damage dealt and regain one expended psi point.

Once you use this action, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 5 Psi Points to use it again.

#### Shattered Husks
Starting at 14th level, your Mind Leech ability always gains the Shredding modifier, and it does not cost a psi point to add the modifier.

Further, you can use additional power to leave their mind further vulnerable. 

You can spend additional psi points on the Shredding modifier to further reduce their next Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw (up to 2 additional points to reduce it by a total of 3d4). 

Once this effect has been applied to a creature, you cannot spend additional psi points on the Shredding modifier against the creature until 1 hour has passed.

### Chrono Mind

Chronomind experience time as another direction through which the mind can move. They steal moments from the future, restore missed opportunities, age matter into dust, and briefly remove creatures from the flow of history.

#### Rapid Manifestation
At 1st level, you accelerate your thoughts until the world appears to slow around you. When you use a psionic power that normally requires an action, you can spend 1 psi point to use it as a bonus action instead.

After using this feature, you can't use another psionic power or cast a spell during the same turn. You can use Rapid Manifestation a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

#### Precognition Discipline
At 2nd level, You cause time around you to shift, speeding up the amount of time it takes to manifest a power. You gain the psionic discipline of Precognition.

#### Momentary Rewind
Starting at 3rd level, you can seize a failed moment and force it to happen again. When you or a creature you can see within 30 feet fails an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can use your reaction and spend 1 psi point to have that creature reroll the d20. The creature must use the new result.

You can use this feature after learning that the original roll failed, but before resolving the consequences of the failure.

#### Decay
At 6th level, you rapidly advance the age of matter. As an action, you can touch a nonmagical object that isn't being worn or carried. A Medium or smaller object crumbles into useless dust. If the object is Large or larger, a 5-foot cube of it crumbles instead. The GM can rule that particularly resilient or plot-critical objects are unaffected.

You can also use this feature against a Creature you touch. Expend 1 or more psi points, up to your proficiency bonus. The Creature makes a Wisdom saving throw against your Psionic save DC, taking 1d10 necrotic damage for each point spent on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. This damage ignores resistance to necrotic damage.

\column

#### Fickle Readiness
At 10th level, you can't be surprised while you are conscious.

When you roll initiative, you can choose one of the following effects:

- One surprised creature you can see within 90 feet is no longer surprised.
- You exchange the initiative results of two willing creatures you can see within 90 feet.
- You exchange your initiative result with that of one creature you can see within 90 feet. An unwilling creature can make a Charisma saving throw against your Psionic save DC, preventing the exchange on a success.

Once you alter initiative with this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a short or long rest.

#### Time Pocket
At 14th level, you temporarily remove a creature from the normal flow of time. As an action, expend 5 psi points and choose a creature you can see within 120 feet. The target makes a Charisma saving throw against your Psionic save DC.

On a failed save, the target takes 6d10 psychic damage and disappears into a temporal pocket. It is incapacitated, can't be targeted or affected, and perceives no passage of time. It reappears at the end of your turn 1d4 + 1 rounds later in its former space or the nearest unoccupied space.

On a successful save, the target takes half as much damage, doesn't disappear, and its speed is halved until the end of its next turn.

Once you use Time Pocket, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Elemental Mind
Psionics and the elements are forces of raw, primal power—and you are the vessel through which they converge. As an Elemental Mind, you channel elemental energy not by incantation or pact, but through pure thought and will. Your psionic potential is bound to the primal chaos of fire, cold, lightning, and force, and your mind becomes a conduit for shaping, warping, and weaponizing these powers in extraordinary ways.

Through intense training and innate connection, you learn to attune your psyche to elemental forces, taking on their properties and embodying their power. Whether lashing out with tendrils of flame, freezing the air around you with a thought, or blinking across the battlefield in a bolt of lightning, your elemental will reshapes the battlefield—and yourself.

Those who witness your powers at work see more than magic: they see the elements themselves made sentient and precise, dancing at the edge of control, as if nature itself had learned to think.



#### Primordial Aspect
Additionally at 1st level, as an action or when you deal fire, cold or lightning damage (no action required), you take on an aspect of that element until the end of your next turn or until you take a new primordial aspect.

- Cold: You gain an icy shell, reducing any non-magical bludgeoning, piercing or slashing damage taken by your proficiency bonus.
- Fire: You gain a fiery aura, once per turn, a creature within 5 feet that hits you with a melee attack takes fire damage equal to your proficiency bonus.
- Lightning: You flicker with lightning. Your walking speed is increased by 5 feet for the duration.


You can additionally spend 1 psi point to gain resistance to the element to the start of your next turn. If you are a specialized in an element, you you can do this without spending a psi point for the element you are specialized in.
{{note
##### Aspect Appearance
The appearance of the aspect manifesting itself may vary, perhaps your hair becomes fire and radiates heat, or your skin takes on an icy sheen, but whatever the nature and consequence of the aspect is apparent to observers.
}}
\column
 
#### Elemental Power
At 2nd level when you select this archetype, you gain a deep intrinsic tie to elemental power, granting you the ability to manipulate it. You gain the psionic discipline of Psychokinetics.



#### Living Power
Starting at 3rd power, your powers become a living extension of your mind, weaving an extension of your will
into reality, allowing you to manipulate your powers in more advanced ways. When you use a power or alternate effect of Psychokinetics, you can apply one of the following modifiers:

- **Shaped Power:** When you use a power, you can convert it to a melee spell attack (such as forming it a weapon shape), and when you would make an attack that would make a melee spell attack, you can instead form it into a shape and hurl it at a target within 15 feet making a ranged spell attack.

- **Controlled Power:** When you use a spell or power that targets an area, you can select a number of creatures equal to the psi points spent in the area of effect for the spell or power to be ignored. The power passes harmlessly around these creatures, they automatically succeed on their saving throw against the effect, and they take no damage if they would normally take half damage on a successful save against the effect.

- **Raging Power:** When you use a spell or power, you can let it rage out of control. When you roll damage for the power or spell, you can reroll 1 + the number of psi points spent on the power or spell dice. You must use the new roll.

#### Empowered Psionics
Starting at 6th level, when you deal damage with a Psionic Discipline power you can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt.
{{pageNumber,auto}}
\page




#### Full Manifestation
Starting at 10th level, when you enter a primordial aspect, you can expend 1 psi point to fully manifest that element, replacing the primordial aspect with the following effect. 

If you are specialized in that element, it does not require a psi point to fully manifest the power.

- Cold: Your movement speed becomes zero until the end of your next turn, but you have resistance to all damage types beside fire and force.

- Fire: Fire fills a 10 foot radius around you. When the creature enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it takes fire damage equal to 1d4 + your proficiency bonus.

- Lightning: You can use your movement speed to teleport a distance equal to your movement speed for the duration.

If you are not in a primordial aspect, you can fully manifest a power as a bonus action for 2 psi points (or 1 psi point if you are specialized in that power).


#### Elemental Form

Starting at 14th level, you can expend 5 psi points to cast *shapechange*, requiring no verbal or material components. When you cast the spell with this feature, you can assume only the form of an Air Elemental, Fire Elemental, or Water Elemental.

If you have an elemental specialization, the cost is reduced to 3 psi points, but you can assume only the form associated with your specialization.

A Water Elemental assumed with this feature doesn't have the Freeze trait.

Once you cast the spell with this feature, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

### Knowing Mind
A Knowing Mind is a psion that grasps that future as
intuitively as the present, weaving together strings of
casualty and fate in ways that would shatter the mind
of a lesser mortal. They live one step ahead and use
that advantage to the fullest, bending probability,
manipulating fate, and bringing their plans to fruition
without room for failure.

They are remarkable individuals whose clairvoyant
abilities stem from a variety of sources—from a
lineage of divine oracles, to a connection to beings
from another temporal axis, to a mind that has
somehow been flung into the future.

#### One step further
Starting at 1st level, your mind bends time and glimpses moments yet to come. While you are not wearing any armor or carrying a shield, your AC equals 13 + your Intelligence modifier.

#### Forewarned
At 2nd level, when you select this archetype, you begin to glimpse the future, granting the psionic discipline
of Precognition.

Additionally, gazing into the future with the Prescience feature of Precognition no longer
requires your concentration.

#### Climactic Moment
Starting at 3rd level, your ability to see and influence the future allows you to set things up to have even
greater influence throughout the sequence of events, building up to a pivotal moment where exerting your foresight can provide the greatest impact.

Once per turn when you use *Seeing* empowered by 1 or psi points to look into the future while in initiative,
you gain an Influence point. 

You can have a maximum number of Influence points equal to your Intelligence modifier.

When you start your turn with one or more Influence points, you can expend all of your Influence points as psi points to empower a psionic power.

Influence points cannot be combined with psi points.

Any generated Influence Points are lost after 1 minute, or when no longer in combat.


{{note
##### Conditions of Influence
You can only build up Influence points while in combat or other tense situations while every action counts as they represent chaining together events toward creating an opportunity. 

Without the pressure and stakes of combat or similar environments, the flow of events cannot be manipulated in such a way.

This generally means you cannot build up Influence points before combat.

Starting at 6th level, you can begin to see the path you must take to bring your plans to fruition. When

power.

#### Window of Opportunity
Starting at 10th level, you can see the flow of battle, giving time to exploit openings and opportunities.

When a creature you can see misses you with an attack, you can use your reaction to make a weapon attack against that target or use a psionic power against them. 

When using a psionic power with this reaction, you cannot expend psi points to empower it, but can use Influence Points.


#### Mastermind
Starting at 14th level, once per turn, when you use your Seeing power, you can select two beneficiaries
of your power. Psi points spent are unique to each target affected (and not shared between them).

to target a second creature, but neither benefits from the

### Illusive Mind
An Illusive Mind is a psion whose power shifts, conceals itself, and evades definition. Their abilities may have been sparked by any event, but manifest in slippery powers to deceive and manipulate others. They share some skills in common with telepaths or shapers, but lie in the grey area in between - rather than manifesting their thoughts as physical objects, Illusive Minds manifest their thoughts in the minds of others.

#### Influential Imagery
At 1st level, you gain the ability to cast minor illusion with your psionic powers. When you cast it to distract or confuse a creature contesting or targeted by a Charisma (Deception), Charisma (Persuasion), or Dexterity (Stealth) check, the creature making the check can add your Intelligence modifier to the roll.


#### Psychic Manipulation
At 2nd level when you select this archetype, you are able to create constructs in the minds of others, granting you the psionic discipline of Mesmerism.



#### Reflected Agony
At 3rd level, your illusions bedevil your foes. When a creature fails a saving throw against one of your psionic powers, you can create a reflection of itself to torment it. At the start of each of your turns, any creature with such a reflection takes psychic damage equal to your proficiency bonus. 

A creature may destroy its reflection as an action, or by expending one attack when it takes the Attack action. A creature may only have one reflection. If not destroyed, a reflection lasts for 1 minute, until the target creature falls unconscious, or until you fall unconscious.

\column
#### Empowered Psionics
Starting at 6th level, when a creature suffers damage from one of your psionic discipline powers you can add your Intelligence modifier to the damage dealt.

#### Illusory Armor
At 10th level, you can construct a set of illusions that gives a creature the appearance of indomitable strength. As an action, you can enrobe one creature you can see within 30 feet in this imaginary armor. While they possess illusory armor, their armor class can't be lower than 22 + your Intelligence modifier. The illusory armor lasts until the target is hit by an attack, after which it vanishes.

Only one creature can possess your Illusory Armor at a time, and once they receive it, they can't receive it again until after a long rest.


#### Army of Illusions
At 14th level, you can conjure a veritable forest of false images, utterly confusing your foes. As an action, choose a point you can see within 60 feet. You create illusory doubles of every creature of your choice within 30 feet of that point. While a target has an illusory double, they gain the following benefits:

- Attack rolls against them have disadvantage.
- They make the first attack roll on their turn each turn at advantage.
- All their attacks deal 1d4 bonus psychic damage.
- A creature's illusory double evaporates when they are hit by a weapon attack, or after 1 minute.

You can create an illusory army in this way once, regaining the ability after a long rest.

### Invasive Mind
Psions of the Invasive Mind enter the thoughts of others as easily as a thief enters an unlocked room. They perceive emotions as patterns, memories as structures, and identity as something that can be edited.

Some Invasive Minds are artificial beings created to understand mortal emotion. Others are natural telepaths who have detached themselves from their own feelings in order to study those of everyone around them.

Their power can expose secrets, replace an enemy’s perception, erase moments of awareness, or imprison a creature inside a fabricated memory.

#### Emotionless Observer

Starting at 1st level, you gain proficiency in the Insight skill.

If you are already proficient in Insight, you gain proficiency in Deception or Investigation instead.

You can use Intelligence in place of Wisdom or Charisma whenever you make an Insight or Deception check.

#### Opened Mind

At 2nd level, your mind awakens the ability to directly enter the thoughts of other creatures, granting you the Telepathy Discipline.

#### Invasion

Starting at 3rd level, whenever a creature fails its saving throw against your Telepathic Intrusion, you can apply one of the following additional effects.

##### Memory Skip

The target can’t take Reactions and is unable to remember your location until the start of your next turn.
You are considered Invisible to the target during that time.
This effect ends early if you deal damage to it.

##### Surface Read

You learn:
The target’s immediate surface thoughts.
Its strongest current emotion.
Whether it knowingly speaks a lie before the end of your next turn.

##### False Signal
The next attack roll made against the target before the start of your next turn has advantage.

\column

#### Rewrite Light

Beginning at 6th level, you can replace what another creature sees with a false image.

When a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, you can use your Reaction and expend 1 Psi Point to force it to make an Intelligence saving throw against your Psionic Ability save DC.

On a failed save, you choose a different creature within the attack’s range as the target of the attack.

If there is no other valid target, the attack misses.

#### Blackout

At 10th level, you can erase awareness from several minds at once.

As an Action, expend 3 Psi Points and choose a point you can see within 60 feet.

Each creature of your choice in a 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on that point must make an Intelligence saving throw.

On a failed save, a creature suffers the following effects until the start of your next turn:

- It is Incapacitated.
- Its speed becomes 0.
- It can’t perceive anything.

When the effect ends, the creature forgets everything that occurred since the end of its previous turn.

Once you use Blackout, you can’t use it again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

#### Lost World

Starting at 14th level, you can imprison a creature inside a complete fabricated memory.

As an Action, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet and expend 5 Psi Points.

The target must make an Intelligence saving throw against your Psionic Ability save DC.

On a failed save, the target is Blinded, Deafened, Incapacitated, and has a speed of 0 for 1 minute as it experiences a false world of your creation.

You must concentrate on this effect as if concentrating on a spell.

At the end of each of its turns, the target repeats the saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

If the target takes damage, it has advantage on the next saving throw it makes against this feature.

While the target remains trapped, you can use a Bonus Action to Deal 4d8 psychic damage to it and alter one minor detail of the fabricated memory.

Once a creature succeeds on its saving throw against Lost World, it is immune to this feature for 24 hours.

Once you use Lost World, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

### Maverick

Mavericks reject formal psionic schools. Instead of perfecting one discipline, they learn to reduce waste, turn expended mental energy into violence, and force any power far beyond its safe specifications.

#### Raw Power
At 1st level, once on each of your turns when a psionic power deals damage and you spent no psi points on that use of the power, add your Intelligence modifier to one damage roll against one target.

In addition, whenever you finish a long rest, choose one Psionic Talent you know. Until your next long rest, you can treat that talent as though it belonged to the discipline granted by this archetype whenever a feature distinguishes between disciplines.

#### Unbound Discipline
At 2nd level, choose one Psionic Discipline that could normally be granted by a Psionic Archetype selected at 2nd level. You must meet every prerequisite for that discipline other than belonging to a specific archetype.

You can't use this feature to select a discipline that has a Psion level prerequisite, is described as an advanced or higher-level discipline, or otherwise wouldn't be available as the first discipline of a newly created Psion. Gaining Psion levels later doesn't remove this restriction.

Starting at 3rd level, once on each of your turns when you spend 2 or more psi points to modify a Psionic Discipline power, you can reduce the total number of psi points expended by 1.

#### Shock Absorption
At 10th level, when you take damage, you can use your reaction and expend 1 psi point to halve the triggering damage. This reduction applies after resistance and vulnerability.

After using Shock Absorption, you have resistance to psychic damage until the start of your next turn.

At 14th level, when you deal damage with a psionic power, you can cause every damage die rolled for that power against one target to deal its maximum value instead of rolling. Any damage dice that recur on later turns are rolled normally.

Once you use Full Force, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

You gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage.

This action can be used only to take the Attack, Dash, or Disengage action.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until the start of your next turn.

### Wandering Mind
A Wandering Mind is among the more mysterious incarnates

### Warped Mind
Some depraved Psions turn their powers on their own body, using it to warp their flesh, twist their bones, and mutate their muscles to give them powers and abilities nature did not see fit to provide. This path of mad self experimentation has resulted in more than one mindless abomination or fleshy pile of gore and goop as such powers run rampant and those that delve this grotesque route are often viewed as monsters--sometimes for good reason. But where there is power, there are practitioners, so there will always be some that ignore the danger, push past the horror, and embrace the madness of these abilities, becoming that which others fear.


#### Rapid Evolution
At 1st level when you select this archetype, your body rapidly develops to different environments. When you complete a long rest in a hostile environment, you adapt to that environment at that end of the long rest. This adaption can one of the following:

**Thermal Adaption.** You adapt to extremely hot or cold weather, no longer requiring extra water or cold weather clothing to sustain yourself in those environments.

**Aquatic Adaption.** You gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed and the ability to breathe underwater.

**Darkness Adaption.** You gain darkvision of 30 feet, or if you already had it, your darkvision increases by 30 feet.

**Inured Immunity.** You become immune to a mundane disease you've been exposed to.

The adaptation must be related to the environment you rest in, and you must not have been exposed to a contradictory element (such as sunlight if you wish to adapt darkvision) for 24 hours. 

At your DM's discretion, you may be able to adapt to more unique or exotic effects that otherwise serve as climate hazards (such as adapting to tune out constant loud noise or motion).

This adaptation lasts until you complete a long rest, at which point the adaptation continues if you remain in the environment, fades if you enter a neutral environment, or changes to a new one if you rest in a different extreme environment. You can only be adapted to one environment at a time. 

Alternatively, you can expend 1 psi point to trigger one of these adaptations forcefully as an action, lasting for 10 minutes when you do so.

#### Metamorphic Mind

At 2nd level, you gain the Biokinesis Discipline.

When you make an attack with a natural weapon created by Weapon Morph, you can use your Intelligence modifier instead of Strength or Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls.

\column

#### Mutation Mastery

Starting at 3rd level, you can manifest multiple Weapon Morph options simultaneously, provided each option affects a different limb. You can also activate Weapon Morph when you roll initiative, requiring no action.

When you use Flesh Warp targeting only yourself, you can use it as a bonus action. When you gain a Mutation Die from Flesh Warp, you also gain temporary hit points equal to one roll of that die.

Your natural weapons created by Weapon Morph count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

Additionally, while you aren't wearing armor, your Armor Class equals 13 + your Constitution modifier. You can use a shield and still gain this benefit.


#### Extra Attack
Beginning at 6th level, you can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.
Moreover, if you use your action to cast a Psionic Power, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action on that turn.


#### Rapid Regeneration

Starting at 10th level, at the start of your turn, if you have at least 1 hit point and no more than half your hit points remaining, you regain hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier.

You can regain hit points with this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

In addition, when you succeed on a death saving throw, you can expend 1 psi point to regain 1d8 hit points.

#### Abomination Unleashed

Starting at 14th level, you can use a bonus action to assume an abominable form for 1 minute. The transformation ends early if you become incapacitated or end it as a bonus action.

For the duration, you gain the following benefits:

- You gain temporary hit points equal to your Psion level when you transform.
- You can manifest every Weapon Morph option simultaneously.
- Your reach with natural weapons increases by 5 feet.
- At the start of each of your turns, choose acid, bludgeoning, cold, fire, lightning, piercing, poison, or slashing damage. You have resistance to the chosen damage type until the start of your next turn.
- When you take the Attack action and attack with a natural weapon, you can make one additional natural weapon attack as part of that action.

Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

physical skill beyond normal mortal limits.

You gain proficiency in Athletics. If you already have
proficiency in Athletics when you select this talent, your
proficiency bonus is doubled for any Athletics check you make.

In addition, standing up from prone costs you only 5 feet of
movement.

#### Kinetic Footwork

You can turn a sudden burst of psionic reinforcement into
explosive movement.

When you use Paragon Surge, you can expend 1 psi point to immediately move up to half your walking speed.

This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

The psi point spent on this talent counts toward the maximum
number of psi points you can spend on that use of Paragon Surge.

#### Impossible Climber

Your hands and feet can exert enough force to carry you across
surfaces that would normally be impossible to traverse.

You gain a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

While Paragon Surge is active, you can move across vertical
surfaces and ceilings during your movement without making an
ability check, provided you end this movement on a surface
capable of supporting you.

If you end your turn on a ceiling or other surface from which
you cannot normally remain suspended, you fall.

#### Perfected Reflexes

Your body reacts to danger almost as quickly as your mind can
perceive it.

When you make a Dexterity saving throw, you can use your
reaction and expend 1 psi point to add your Intelligence
modifier to the roll.

You can use this talent after rolling the d20, but before the

#### Psionic Might

*Prerequisite: 5th-level Psion*::

Your body can briefly exert truly superhuman strength.

*Prerequisite: 11th-level Psion*::

Projection is the ability to project what is in your mind to the outside world with your psionic power.

As an action, you can use your powers to project an inanimate object you imagine into your hands. It cannot be larger than 3 feet on any side or weigh more than 10 pounds, and is clearly ethereal in nature. 

The item behaves as a solid object. Weapons created with this feature deal force damage. Projected items fade after 1 minute, and you can have no more than 3 projected items at a time.

*Psionic power*
**Casting Time:**:: 1 action
**Range:**:: 60 feet
**Components:**:: S
**Duration:**:: Concentration, up to 1 minute

This creation is clearly ethereal in nature.

If the Astral Construct moves out of range of the spell, it fades away into nothing, ending the spell.

**Relocate (1 psi point):** The construct disappears and reappears anywhere within 60 feet of you.

**Solidify (1 psi point):** Your construct becomes solid, blocking its space until the start of your next turn. You can only use this command if it is in an unoccupied space. It has an armor class of 16 and hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier + your psion level. The construct becomes ethereal again if it drops
to 0 hit points.

**Grow (1 psi point):** Your construct increases by one size, and its number of damage dice increases by one. It returns to its normal size at the start of your next turn. It can only gain one additional die (for example, up to 2d8).

**Components:**:: S
**Duration:**:: Instantaneous

You touch a willing creature and use your mind to jump-start their body's natural repair mechanisms. The target regains 1d4 hit points.

You and other creatures can only benefit from this effect a number of times equal to their Constitution modifier, after which they gain a level of Exhaustion for each time it is used on them until they complete a long rest, resetting how many times they can use it.

You can spend Psi Points up to your per use limit to add the following modifiers to Bio-Feedback (you can add multiple modifiers). The points must be spent when choosing the target of the power.

**Restorative (1+ psi points).** The target regains an additional 1d4 hit points for each point spent.

**Adrenal (1 psi point).** You awaken the target's fight response. The target can use their reaction to make one weapon attack or move up to their speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Purging (2 psi points).** End one disease or one of the following conditions affecting the target: blinded, deafened, or poisoned.

**Broadcast (2 psi points).** The range of the power becomes 30 feet.

**Limitless (3 psi points).** The target gains resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the start of your next turn.

### Vitalism Talents
The following talents can be selected if you have the Vitalism Discipline:

#### Biotic Echo 

Your connection to the flow of life allows you to siphon excess energy back into yourself. When you restore hit points to a creature other than yourself using a Psionic Power, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.

#### Emergency Feedback
*Prerequisite: 5th-level Psion*

When a creature you can see within 30 feet is reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction and expend 1 psi point to use Bio-Feedback on that creature.

The range of Bio-Feedback becomes 30 feet for this use, and you can apply its modifiers as normal.

Once you use this talent, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

#### Instinctual Awakening 
*Prerequisite: 5th level Psion*::

You can tap into the dormant survival instincts of an ally, forcing their body to react before their mind can process the danger. When a creature you can see within 30 feet of you makes a Dexterity or Constitution saving throw, you can use your reaction and expend 2 psi points to grant them advantage on the roll. If they succeed on the save, they also gain temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier.

#### Psychosomatic Ward

When you restore hit points to a creature using Bio-Feedback, you can choose to grant them Temporary Hit Points equal to the amount healed instead of restoring actual Hit Points.

#### Remote Monitoring

The range of your Vital Awareness feature increases to 60 feet, and you can communicate telepathically with any willing creature within that range.


#### Revitalizing Surge

*Prerequisite: 9th level Psion*::

You gain the ability to cast *revivify* by expending 3 psi points. 
Once you cast it this way, you cannot cast it again until you complete a long rest.
